HORIZON V1.99.150 - WHATSAPP AUTO-REPLY: FAILURES EXPLAINED + 3 FIXES
What the screenshots showed (log, 06:15-06:17): 1 "replied", 4 "ai-failed", and a row from "You" that was Horizon's own reply coming back as if someone sent it.
Findings in the code:
1. "ai-failed" never said why. Every failure path returned null silently. The four failures each landed 1-2 seconds apart, so they were NOT timeouts: the provider (or a guard) rejected them fast.
2. The card hint "Select a supported AI provider first" is real: the native bot cannot call Horizon Worker 1/2/3 (the worker URL lives in the web layer, not in the Kotlin provider list). The bot uses whichever supported provider was saved when you switched it on, not the one you are chatting with now.
3. A reasoning model (deepseek-r1-distill) writes <think>...</think> first. Nothing stripped it, so a contact could have received the model's thinking, or the 512-token cap could be eaten by thinking and leave nothing.
4. WhatsApp re-posts your reply as a notification from "You". The bot logged it and tried to answer itself (also burns the hourly cap).
Fixes (HorizonWaBotPlugin.kt only):
- The log now says why: ai-failed: http-429 / http-5xx / network:<error> / model-substituted:<name> / empty-reply / no-endpoint / provider-or-model-not-allowed.
- One polite retry after 6 s on http-429, http-5xx or a network error (queue is single-file, so no stampede).
- <think> blocks are stripped; unfinished thinking is dropped, never sent to a person.
- Notifications from "You", and text identical to something Horizon just sent, are ignored.
Not changed: provider choice, the system prompt, caps, allowlist, STOP/START.
Tests: tests/wabot-v137.test.cjs 9/9 pass (static checks).
NOT CHECKED: no Kotlin compile, no APK build, no phone run, no real WhatsApp message. The real cause of the 4 failures is still unknown until the log shows the new reason.
