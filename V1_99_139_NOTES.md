HORIZON V1.99.139 - NOTIFICATION ALERT HARDENING, PLAY-NEXT QUEUE, PLAYLIST FIXES, FIX MUSIC INFO

1. Notification alert: HorizonNotify.alert and publishReply now fall back to a plain banner if the rich one (full-screen intent, Reply button, active-notification listing) throws. Previously any exception dropped the alert silently.
2. Queue: [[music.queue]] used to append to the END while saying "Queued next". It now inserts right after the current song (works with shuffle). New [[music.add]] = end of queue. [[yt.queue]] also plays next. Playlists still queue in order.
3. Playlists: [[playlist.create]] accepts sloppy AI output (curly quotes, trailing commas, code fence, plain list), makes a duplicate name unique instead of failing, trims lists over 30, and playback skips songs it cannot find instead of abandoning the whole list. The music prompt now teaches the AI queue and playlist tags.
4. Fix music info (new www/horizon_tagfix.js): button "Fix music info" in the Music tab, or the AI tag [[music.fixtags]]. Fills missing title/artist/album and artwork (iTunes Search) and saves lyrics (LRCLIB) inside the app. Confident matches only. Song files are not rewritten.
