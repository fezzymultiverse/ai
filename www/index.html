 Proactive Daemon

                            
Checking

                        

                    

                    
Watches the battery while Horizon is running. When it falls to your level and the phone is not charging, Horizon pauses the music, stops reading aloud if it was, and pops a banner that says what it did. Android hides real CPU load from apps, so Horizon watches the phone's heat warning instead. If you hammer the backspace key, it asks "Need help?" and a tap lets you talk instead of type. It cannot see typing in other apps, and it does not run if Android has closed Horizon.

Equalizer · works with every voice

                            
Dynamics and cleanup

                            
Creative effects

                        

                        
                        
22 presets, including 2 natural English voices and 10 added character transformations. Android synthesizes to a private file; only the processed voice plays when effects are enabled. EQ stays editable after any preset or voice selection and applies on the next playback. Character mix blends transformed and original timbre. Six added processors: hard limiter, peak normalize, humanize, de-esser, noise gate and warm saturation. No exact voice cloning or system-wide voice replacement. Neutral bypasses rendering.
