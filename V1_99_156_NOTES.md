Only android-native/app/HorizonWaBotPlugin.kt changed (the WhatsApp parts of the V1.99.161 zip, applied as a patch):
- A WhatsApp chat entry with no sender info is no longer treated as your own message (this made the bot ignore real incoming messages). Own-message detection now uses the saved user key, the name "You", and the echo list of replies the bot already sent.
- Only reply buttons that accept typed text (free-form RemoteInput) are used.
- The AI is told to answer the latest message using the recent chat, to vary its wording, and not to reuse a fixed away message.

- Switching the bot off while the AI is thinking, or during the send delay, now cancels the reply.
- Replies are capped at 540 characters (was 900).
