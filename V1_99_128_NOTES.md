HORIZON V1.99.128 - PERSONA HOLD

Problem: the AI said "I don't have feelings" and other stock assistant lines while still half-acting the persona.
Two causes found in www/index.html:
1. Small models (AI Horde, Cloudflare small models, anything 1b-8b, e2b/e4b, lora) get getLitePrompt(), and that prompt had NO persona at all: just "You are HORIZON... be short". With nothing to hold on to, they fell back to default assistant talk.
2. On the full prompt the persona sits at the very top, under the tool contracts and every module block (thousands of characters), so the model's default voice wins by the end.

Fix:
- New liteSoul(): small models now get a compact persona (or your custom persona, cut to 900 chars).
- New personaHold(): a short "STAY IN CHARACTER" block placed LAST in both the lite and the full prompt. It bans stock lines like "As an AI, I don't have feelings / I'm just a language model", tells the model to show care through what it says, and does not name a character, so a custom persona from Settings still wins.
- If you sincerely ask what it is or whether it feels something, it answers that one question honestly and briefly, in voice, then carries on. It never claims to be human.

Files: www/index.html (helpers + two push lines + cache versions), www/horizon_addons.js (cache version), package.json, package-lock.json.
Checked: inline JS passes node --check; helpers unit-run. tests/chatbox-v55.test.cjs already failed before this (it hard-codes v1.99.99); not caused by this change.
